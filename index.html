MEMORANDUM: COMMERCIAL STRATEGY & PILOT RECOMMENDATION

To: Executive Committee & Commercial Leadership, Meesho (Fashnear Technologies) From: E-Commerce Commercial Strategy Engagement Team Subject: Unit Economics Turnaround & Controlled Pilot Protocol ("Project BharatPulse")

1. Executive Summary & Core Strategic Insight

Meesho should NOT execute an unvalidated national rollout of Lever 4 (₹8.30 Cr combined capital push) or Lever 1 (unilateral buyer platform fee). Our quantitative decision model establishes that:

  • Lever 1 (Platform Fee) triggers heavy customer churn: Tier-2/3 price elasticity ($E_d = -1.45$) results in a loss of ~47.1M annual orders, destroying seller density and organic marketplace liquidity.
  • Lever 2 (Valmo Logistics) and Lever 3 (Seller Ads) face operational lags: Driver onboarding, pin-code mapping, and ad wallet funding create a 3 to 4 month drag where CapEx is unabsorbed.
  • Lever 4 (Combined Full Push) bears severe capital risk: Committing ₹8.30 Cr upfront carries negative Year-1 returns when accounting for the 25% overlap damping penalty.

2. Primary Strategic Recommendation: Execute "Project BharatPulse" (₹2.25 Cr Pilot)

Proposed Pilot Protocol — "Project BharatPulse"
Geography: Eastern UP & Bihar Cluster (Purvanchal: Varanasi, Gorakhpur, Patna hubs)
