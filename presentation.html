Direct Monetization vs Price Sensitivity in Low-Income Cohorts

The Consulting "Plot Twist": Why Big Bang Year-1 Fails

The Execution Paradox of Ramp Friction & Capital Exposure

Strategic Tension

Why Big Bang Rollouts Underperform

  • Ramp-Up Friction Lag: Micro-depot hubs, 3PL contract transitions, and driver onboarding require 4 months of operational stabilization.
  • Lumpy Capital Burden: In Months 1–3, CapEx amortization precedes volume efficiencies, depressing net cash flows.
  • Irreversible Consumer Pushback: Introducing checkout fees nationally risks permanent cohort attrition to Shopsy and Amazon Bazaar.

The Consulting Truth

Do not deploy full-scale capital against unvalidated behavioral elasticity.

The optimal corporate strategy is not to jump immediately to Lever 4, but to create a Controlled Regional Option that limits downside risk while acquiring empirical operational data.

The Real Option Principle: Information has quantifiable commercial value. Sizing a regional test reduces capital at risk by 73% while preserving 100% of the strategic upside.
